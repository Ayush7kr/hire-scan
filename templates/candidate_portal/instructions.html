Assessment Rules & Guidelines
  • Authoritative Server Timer: The countdown timer begins the instant you click "Start Assessment". The server maintains the master clock. If your timer reaches 00:00, your test will finalize automatically.

  • Integrity Monitoring: Tab switching and browser minimizes are tracked as audit signals. Please remain on the test page in full screen until completion.
